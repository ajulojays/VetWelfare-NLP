The platform

From raw records to trusted veterinary AI assets.

VetAnnotate combines collaborative annotation, data standardization, quality control and benchmark creation in one secure workflow.

01

Ingest

Import clinical notes, diagnostic reports, imaging metadata, laboratory records and research datasets using institution-approved connectors.

02

Standardize

Map records to shared veterinary ontologies, terminology standards and common data models without erasing local meaning.

03

04

Governance by design

Collaboration without surrendering institutional control.

VetAnnotate is designed for federated participation. Institutions decide what can be used, where data reside, how access is granted and which outputs may be released.

Local stewardship

Data ownership and stewardship remain with contributing institutions.

Role-based access

Annotators, adjudicators, investigators and administrators receive only the permissions they need.

Full provenance

Every annotation, revision, adjudication and model-assisted suggestion is auditable.

Responsible release

Only approved derived datasets, benchmarks and metadata are shared publicly.

Program roadmap

From collaborative annotation to a national veterinary AI ecosystem.

Phase I

Foundation

Launch VetAnnotate, establish shared governance, recruit founding institutions and release initial benchmark datasets.

Phase II

Federation

Connect diagnostic and clinical data environments through interoperable standards and institution-controlled workflows.

Phase III

Foundation models

Train and validate VetWelfare-NLP, ParasiteFM, VetPathFM, VetGenomeFM and VetRadiologyFM.

Phase IV

Center-scale translation

Establish a multi-institutional Center for Veterinary AI Innovation supporting research, training, validation and deployment.

Our vision
Veterinary medicine should not be limited by fragmented data. VetAnnotate creates the shared infrastructure needed to transform clinical and diagnostic knowledge into trustworthy AI, stronger evidence and better care for animals and people.
